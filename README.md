# ROAST 🔥

**Your landing page, graded honestly.**

Paste your landing page copy → get a brutally honest conversion teardown in 30 seconds.

- **Letter grade** (A+ to F) across 6 dimensions: Clarity, Specificity, Proof, Hook, CTA, Hygiene
- **Specific flags** — buzzword count, dead CTAs, missing proof, headline length
- **3 rewritten headlines** — pain→outcome, specific proof, direct benefit
- **Shareable result** — copy your roast or share to X

No signup. No API. No tracking. Runs entirely in your browser.

## Why rule-based?

The analysis engine is deterministic — it scores against proven copywriting principles (buzzword density, headline length, proof signals, CTA strength). No LLM, no API keys, no server. Instant results, total privacy.
